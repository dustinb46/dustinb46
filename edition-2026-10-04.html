Why it matters: Producers enrolled at the $9.50 coverage level have an August payment coming for the first time since February, and the three-month slope means the budgeting question is no longer whether DMC pays but for how many months.

Federally pooled milk ran 5.3% above year-ago in August — and the uniform price still fell

06 · Animal Health

Idaho now has 18 dairies under H5N1 quarantine spread across nine counties

The Idaho State Department of Agriculture's H5N1 cattle page, updated September 30, lists 18 currently quarantined dairy facilities: Canyon and Gooding four each, Owyhee three, Twin Falls two, and Ada, Cassia, Franklin, Jerome and Payette one apiece. That geography matters — the outbreak is no longer confined to the Magic Valley dairy belt but is now running through southwestern and eastern counties as well. The state attributes transmission to a mix of cow-to-cow contact, fomites on boots, trucks and equipment, housing and ventilation, and continued wild bird exposure, and notes the primary economic damage is on-farm production loss. Lactating cattle on affected premises are quarantined, and additional import requirements apply to lactating dairy cattle entering the state. Why it matters: Nine counties with standing quarantines means the exposure risk now travels with routine cattle movement and shared equipment across most of southern Idaho, and anyone sourcing lactating animals from the state should expect added import paperwork.

09 · Policy

The $100 million organic transition program ended September 30 with no successor funding

USDA's Transition to Organic Partnership Program, which put $100 million into technical assistance and mentoring for transitioning and existing organic farmers from 2022 through 2026, closed on September 30. Participating organizations say they intend to keep supporting transitioning farmers, and Arizona State University's Swette Center launched a follow-on mentorship program called Kernel on October 1 — but neither carries the federal funding. Separately, the National Organic Program has a Federal Register notice open on extending an existing information collection, with comments accepted through November 24. Why it matters: Anyone midway through a three-year organic transition just lost the free technical assistance that was built into the plan, and the replacement is a university mentorship program rather than funded federal support.
